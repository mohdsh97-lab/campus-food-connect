About us

A stronger campus starts with connection.

A student proposal linking responsible food recovery, everyday prevention, and dignified support.

Our mission

To reduce campus food waste and improve access to meals by connecting students, food businesses, and established food assistance partners.

Where we fit.

Food pantries offer essential support. Food recovery groups redirect surplus. Our proposed niche brings these efforts together around college students, with one place to understand the issue and explore participation.

We would complement existing services. Partnerships, food handling arrangements, and any distribution would need to be agreed before the program begins.

What guides us

Practical. Respectful. Accountable.

Respect for students

Financial hardship should never define someone’s worth. We would listen to students without asking them to share their struggles publicly.

Responsible recovery

Only suitable, unserved surplus would be considered. Food from consumers’ plates would never be redistributed.

Honest impact

We would measure progress and report limitations. We do not claim existing partnerships, recovered meals, or results.

The identity

Less Waste.
More Meals.

Our circular plate mark represents food, while its connecting lines reflect the people who make support possible. Our name keeps both the campus community and the connection at the center.
